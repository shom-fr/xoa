.. _references:

References
##########

The methods that xoa implements are described in the following publications.

.. _ref.penven2014:

**Penven et al. (2014)**
    Penven, P., Halo, I., Pous, S., and Marié, L.: Cyclogeostrophic balance in the Mozambique
    Channel, *Journal of Geophysical Research: Oceans*, 119, 1054-1067,
    `doi:10.1002/2013JC009528 <https://doi.org/10.1002/2013JC009528>`_, 2014.
    Used by :func:`xoa.dyn.get_cyclogeos`.

.. _ref.okubo1970:

**Okubo (1970)**
    Okubo, A.: Horizontal dispersion of floatable particles in the vicinity of velocity
    singularities such as convergences, *Deep Sea Research and Oceanographic Abstracts*,
    17, 445-454, `doi:10.1016/0011-7471(70)90059-8 <https://doi.org/10.1016/0011-7471(70)90059-8>`_, 1970.
    Used by :func:`xoa.dyn.get_okuboweiss`.

.. _ref.weiss1991:

**Weiss (1991)**
    Weiss, J.: The dynamics of enstrophy transfer in two-dimensional hydrodynamics,
    *Physica D: Nonlinear Phenomena*, 48, 273-294,
    `doi:10.1016/0167-2789(91)90088-Q <https://doi.org/10.1016/0167-2789(91)90088-Q>`_, 1991.
    Used by :func:`xoa.dyn.get_okuboweiss`.
